The IZEM app is awaiting store review. You can use the free workout generator on this website now.

IZEM connects personalized weekly workouts, training logs, meal plans, grocery lists, progress, AI text chat and eligible live voice calls around one profile. Optional food, equipment and body-progress scans add non-medical coaching context.

When offered, an eligible account can start a 7-day free trial only if the storefront confirms a zero-price one-week offer and account eligibility. When offered during onboarding, an eligible non-subscriber can try one complimentary coach call, once, capped at three connected minutes. An eligible trial includes up to 15 voice minutes total; a paid monthly subscription includes up to 300 live voice-coaching minutes per usage cycle, with individual calls capped at 15 minutes.

Optional coach-initiated calls depend on account eligibility, platform support, available allowance, device permissions and connectivity.

On supported iPhones, separately permitted Apple Health context is limited to user-started text coaching and explicitly requested workout changes. Voice calls do not receive Apple Health context. Health and scan information supports general fitness coaching, not diagnosis or medical treatment.

App availability and membership offers remain subject to store review and the applicable storefront’s terms. IZEM does not guarantee training outcomes or replace a qualified professional.
