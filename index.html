IZEM: AI fitness coaching that plans, tracks, chats, calls, remembers and adapts

IZEM is an adult-only AI fitness coaching app built around one connected profile. It creates personalized weekly workouts and meal plans; tracks sets, reps, load, partial workouts, meals, weight and streaks; generates grocery lists; supports food, equipment and body-progress scans; provides AI text chat; and supports live two-way coach calls.

At the end of onboarding, an eligible account can start the 7-day free trial or test one complimentary coach call first. The test call is capped at 5 minutes. The current IZEM Elite paywall advertises up to 350 live voice-coaching minutes each month, with focused calls up to 15 minutes.

Premium members can start a coach call, while optional coach-initiated calls can support workout accountability and review moments when enabled and eligible. Trial eligibility is read from the storefront rather than promised universally.

On supported iPhones, separately permitted Apple Health context can include available steps and exercise minutes, sleep, workouts, resting heart rate, HRV and body mass. Health and scan information is used for general fitness coaching, not diagnosis or medical treatment.

IZEM does not publish fabricated reviews, invented download counts or guaranteed outcomes. Storefront availability, pricing, renewal terms, call allowances and purchase eligibility should be verified in the current App Store or Google Play offer shown to the user.
