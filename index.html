Questions about IZEM

What is IZEM?

IZEM is an AI personal trainer app for adults. It connects personalized workouts, meal plans, training logs, progress, AI chat and eligible voice calls in one profile.

How does IZEM adapt my workouts?

IZEM builds weekly plans around your goals, experience, equipment, schedule and session length. Logged workouts and feedback can guide progression suggestions. You can also make supported exercise swaps and workout-day changes.

Can the AI coach call me?

You can enable optional calls around workouts and day reviews. Premium members can also call the coach from the app. Calls depend on account eligibility, platform support, available minutes, preferences, device permissions and connectivity.

Does IZEM help with meals and food photos?

Meal plans use your dietary preferences, allergies, meal count, and calorie and macro targets. The active plan also generates a grocery list. IZEM can estimate calories and macros from food photos, but estimates can be wrong. Check ingredients, especially if you have allergies or medical dietary needs.
