## Change

The page was rebuilt in place around a specific beginner-gym decision task:

1. What is the first exercise and setup?
2. What equipment is available right now?
3. What substitute preserves the workout's role?
4. What fallback saves the session when time disappears?
5. How should next week adapt after misses?

The rehabilitation added:

- a browser-only **Beginner Gym Fit Test** scorecard;
- the original **START** framework for first-week gym planning;
- a comparison table for weak generator behavior versus better coaching behavior;
- a worked example of a crowded/late first gym week;
- explicit safety boundaries for pain, injury, form coaching, heavy lifts, and medical needs;
- a CDC source for general adult activity context;
- a visible gym training image from existing IZEM assets and an updated OG card;
- current IZEM positioning around calls, day reviews, practical meals, scans, coach memory, and weekly adaptation;
- a natural premium price anchor of around $24.99/month;
- updated title, meta description, Open Graph, Twitter metadata, Article/FAQ/Breadcrumb JSON-LD, canonical, and `dateModified`.
