The exact private query strings remain encrypted and are not repeated here. This target URL was not receiving visible aggregate impressions and was quarantined with `noindex` because of legacy AI-assisted content risks.

## Why this page won the evening slot

Today's new content asset already targeted progressive overload, so a second progressive-overload page would have created unnecessary same-day cluster pressure. The crowded-gym page supports a different visible cluster: equipment-aware coaching and gym-floor adaptation.

Live SERP review for crowded-gym adaptation queries showed a reachable task gap. Results were mostly broad workout app roundups, gym crowd meters, generic alternatives lists, and forum posts from users asking for an app that adapts when machines are busy. Few results answered the full product task: preserve the workout purpose, account for equipment availability, shrink the session when time is short, and adapt future plans after repeated peak-hour friction.

## Cannibalization check

The rehabilitated URL owns full-session crowded-gym adaptation.

- `/blog/workout-app-exercise-substitutions-busy-machines` owns one unavailable station and the Keep-the-Job Swap Card.
- `/blog/gym-equipment-scanner-workout-app` owns scanner setup, correction, persistence, and the scan-to-workout handoff.
- `/blog/gym-machines-vs-free-weights` owns equipment choice by function, fit, floor access, and follow-through.
- `/blog/best-fitness-app-for-beginners` owns beginner structure, not crowded-gym rescue decisions.

## Information gain

The page adds the Crowded-Gym Rescue Map:

1. Keep the planned movement when it is worth waiting.
2. Swap the equipment while preserving the movement's job.
3. Shrink the session when time runs out.
4. Move the work and adapt the next plan when peak-hour friction repeats.

It also adds a copyable coach prompt, an app-evaluation table, clear product limits, and safer IZEM positioning around calls, day reviews, equipment context, and weekly adaptation.
