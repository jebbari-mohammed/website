No material SEO/content commit had landed on 2026-08-20 before this experiment. The progressive-overload app guide itself was materially rehabilitated on 2026-08-16 and remains inside its content cooldown, so this experiment does not rewrite it.

## SERP rationale

Live Google results for progressive-overload tracker/template/spreadsheet intent include:

- simple free workout-log templates centered on set-level load and reps;
- trainer-oriented Google Sheets templates with client/program fields;
- paid Excel/Google Sheets progressive-overload trackers;
- broader hypertrophy/program builders that export spreadsheet-ready training plans.

The task gap is a no-signup, browser-only progression log that keeps the next-session target explicit, stores data locally, exports clean CSV, and does not pretend a universal algorithm should choose the next load.

## Cannibalization check

No existing indexable IZEM URL satisfies the same template/log task.

- `/blog/best-app-to-track-progressive-overload-automatically` serves app-comparison/evaluation intent.
- `/1rm-calculator/` estimates a one-rep max; it is not a set-by-set progression log.
- `/workout-consistency-calculator/` measures planned versus completed sessions; it does not track exercise progression.
- `/blog/ai-workout-generator-beginners` serves beginner plan-generator evaluation intent.

## Information gain

The page adds:

1. A browser-only, local-storage progressive-overload log with one row per working set.
2. Fields for date, exercise, set, load, reps, optional effort/RIR, context notes, and a manually chosen next-session target.
3. CSV export and copy-to-clipboard without signup or server-side storage.
4. A compact LAST review: Log, Assess, Set, Test.
5. A column-by-column decision table explaining when fields are useful and when optional fields can stay blank.
6. A worked example that explicitly avoids auto-prescribing a weight increase from incomplete context.
7. A format comparison among browser template, spreadsheet, app, and qualified coach.
8. A careful training-scope boundary linked to ACSM's 2026 resistance-training guidance on consistency and individualization.

## Hypothesis

Because Google is already testing IZEM's progressive-overload cluster, a distinct free tracker/template utility can expand that cluster into spreadsheet/template intent without rewriting the recently rehabilitated app-comparison URL. A useful no-signup tool should be more linkable and task-complete than another generic explanatory article.
