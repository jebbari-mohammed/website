# Owner-requested homepage presentation refinement

The owner requested a less generic design below the opening film, particularly its colors and shadows, and explicitly requested a safe skill search and installation through Edge. This is presentation work, not a keyword selection, content launch or ranking experiment.

The opening film remains the first screen. The content below it now uses neutral paper surfaces, dark ink, restrained green controls, balanced Hanken Grotesk type, and aligned feature/evidence rows. Repeated glass cards, ornamental gradients, glows and pill badges were removed. Bounded surfaces are reserved for the product recording, call example and calculator. Primary attention goes to the product explanation and next action, then the real app preview, then supporting facts.

The owner explicitly changed the visual direction of the existing homepage. Existing film, download and FAQ locks remain intact; none of their protected source files or lock definitions was edited.
