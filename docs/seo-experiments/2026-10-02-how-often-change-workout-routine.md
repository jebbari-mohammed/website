# Routine-change Keep–Tune–Replace launch — 2026-10-02

## Hypothesis
A dedicated routine-change timing guide can earn qualified informational visibility without cannibalizing IZEM's existing workout-generator or missed-target pages if it answers a different job: deciding whether to keep, tune, or replace a routine.

## Intent and evidence
Primary user task: decide how often a workout routine should change and whether the current plan actually needs replacement.

Current US keyword research confirmed meaningful demand for routine-change timing. Live Google results were dominated by informational guidance, discussion results, an AI Overview, and fixed-week answers. Repository overlap review found no dedicated English URL for routine-change timing.

Primary sources checked: Baz-Valle et al. (2019), Kassiano et al. (2022), and the 2026 ACSM resistance-training position stand.

## Product fit and information gain
Canonical product facts support adaptation around schedule, equipment, training level, goals, recovery, consistency, and plan changes. The article makes no exact-price, medical-outcome, or competitor-superiority claim.

The page introduces a Keep–Tune–Replace test: keep when the plan still fits, tune when one assumption broke, and replace when several core assumptions no longer match the user's goal or real week.
