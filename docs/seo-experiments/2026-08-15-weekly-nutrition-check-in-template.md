# SEO Experiment: Weekly Nutrition Check-In Template

- **Status:** launched
- **Launch date:** 2026-08-15
- **Target URL:** https://youraicoach.life/blog/weekly-nutrition-check-in-template
- **Primary intent:** weekly nutrition check-in template
- **Secondary intent:** meal planning check-in template; nutrition accountability check-in
- **Action class:** new interactive search-entry asset
- **Owner:** ChatGPT senior SEO lead

## First-party baseline

The 2026-08-15 Search Console health run returned 22 query + landing-page rows, 29 impressions, and 0 clicks for 2026-07-18 through 2026-08-14. Exact queries remain private and are not printed by the public workflow, so this experiment does not pretend GSC exposed this specific query.

URL Inspection returned 5 of 11 monitored URLs indexed, 6 neutral/unknown/stale states, and 0 API errors. The site is still in cold-start exploration, but first-party visibility is increasing from 17 impressions on the prior day to 29 impressions in the freshest 28-day pull.

## SERP rationale

Live search for weekly nutrition check-in and meal-planning accountability variants showed a fragmented result set rather than one dominant self-use answer. Results included a general gym-progress weekly check-in, a paid guide that contains a nutrition check-in, meal-planning/accountability services, and generic planning resources. The gap is a focused, no-signup, browser-only self-review that helps a user identify meal-planning friction without demanding calorie, macro, weight, or medical inputs.

USDA MyPlate also provides current first-party guidance on meal planning around what food is available, writing meals down, available preparation time, leftovers, and grocery-list planning. The new asset uses those logistics as reference points rather than prescribing a diet.

## Cannibalization check

No existing IZEM URL targets the exact weekly nutrition check-in/template task. Nearby pages satisfy different intents:

- `/blog/weekly-fitness-check-in-template`: workout/accountability review intent.
- `/blog/workout-accountability-calendar`: monthly workout tracking/calendar intent.
- `/blog/workout-accountability-agreement-template`: commitment/agreement intent.
- `/blog/adaptive-workout-and-meal-plan-app`: product/evaluation intent and currently quarantined from indexing.
- `/blog/food-scanner-app-with-meal-planning-and-workout-coaching`: product/evaluation intent and currently quarantined from indexing.

## Information gain

The page adds:

1. A browser-only weekly nutrition check-in generator.
2. The IZEM BITE framework: Baseline, Interruptions, Tweak, Execute.
3. A copy-and-paste text template.
4. A logistics decision table for grocery, prep-time, schedule, cost, preference, and rigidity problems.
5. A worked example that diagnoses a grocery-list bottleneck without prescribing nutrients or calories.
6. Clear scope boundaries for clinical nutrition, eating disorders, pregnancy, and other professional-care needs.
7. Direct links to current USDA MyPlate and Dietary Guidelines resources.
