# SEO Experiment: Workout Accountability Calendar

- **Status:** launched
- **Launch date:** 2026-08-14
- **Target URL:** https://youraicoach.life/blog/workout-accountability-calendar
- **Primary intent:** workout accountability calendar
- **Secondary intent:** printable workout accountability calendar; workout accountability tracker; monthly workout accountability calendar
- **Action class:** new interactive search-entry asset
- **Owner:** ChatGPT senior SEO lead

## First-party baseline

The 2026-08-14 Search Console health run returned 13 query + landing-page rows, 17 impressions, and 0 clicks for 2026-07-17 through 2026-08-13. Exact query strings are intentionally not printed by the public workflow, so this experiment does not pretend that GSC exposed this exact keyword.

URL Inspection reported 5 of 11 monitored URLs indexed, with 6 neutral/unknown/stale states and zero API errors. The two newest template experiments remain unknown to Google, which is expected this soon after publication and is not a reason to rewrite them during their cooldown windows.

## SERP rationale

Live search for workout accountability calendar / printable tracker variants is fragmented. Results include a low-engagement Scribd document, generic downloadable workout calendars, spreadsheet/PDF template sites, old blog printables, and paid marketplace products. The dominant task is planning and visually tracking workouts; most results require a download, spreadsheet copy, marketplace purchase, or static printable.

The opportunity is a no-signup browser tool that creates the month immediately, distinguishes planned/completed/missed/rest states, calculates a simple completion summary, persists locally, and prints or saves to PDF without collecting the user's entries.

## Cannibalization check

No existing IZEM URL targets the monthly calendar task. Nearby pages have materially different jobs:

- `/blog/weekly-fitness-check-in-template`: retrospective seven-day review and adjustment.
- `/blog/workout-accountability-agreement-template`: pre-commitment rules and accountability agreement.
- `/workout-consistency-calculator/`: longer-period planned-versus-completed calculation.
- `/blog/workout-accountability-ideas-alone`: broad accountability tactics.

The new page links to these assets as complementary tools rather than re-answering their primary intent.

## Information gain

The page adds:

1. A real monthly calendar generated for the selected month.
2. Four explicit states: planned, completed, missed, and rest.
3. Browser-only local persistence with a clear privacy disclosure.
4. A print / Save PDF mode that isolates the calendar.
5. A live completed/planned/missed summary.
6. The IZEM PACE framework: Plan, Act, Check, Edit.
7. A decision table for interpreting recurring calendar patterns.
8. A worked example showing how a recurring schedule conflict should change the next week's plan.

## Hypothesis

A free, immediate, printable workout accountability calendar with interactive states and no signup will earn discovery, impressions, and clicks for accountability-calendar/tracker searches while attracting users whose consistency problem is closely aligned with IZEM's proactive coaching product.
