## Live SERP context

Live search review for workout-reminder and call-accountability language showed a fragmented result set: app-store reminder utilities, exercise alert products, multi-channel reminder pages, social or partner accountability products, and high-cost human coaching/accountability pages. That creates a useful gap for a page that clearly answers the shopper's decision: when does a call beat an alarm, and when is a simple reminder enough?

The page was already indexed and mature. Creating a new URL would have increased cannibalization risk with `/fitness-app-that-calls-you/`, `/features/ai-voice-calls`, and adjacent accountability pages. Refreshing the existing reminder-intent owner is cleaner.

## Change launched

The refresh keeps the same URL, canonical, robots directive, and article purpose. It updates:

- title, meta description, Open Graph, Twitter, BlogPosting headline/description, and `dateModified`;
- H1 and lead to frame the page as "calls vs alarms";
- the mechanism comparison table with SMS/email/WhatsApp reminders and social accountability alongside alarms, recorded calls, AI coaching calls, and human trainers;
- the link-worthy hook from a generic rescue contract to the clearer One-Call Rule;
- body links into `/features/ai-voice-calls` and `/blog/daily-fitness-check-in-app`.

No new blog post was published. That preserves the weekly creation cap and avoids adding a duplicate call/reminder URL.

## Hypothesis

If Google is already testing the target page near the top for reminder/call intent, a clearer calls-vs-alarms title, snippet, and first-screen answer should increase qualified CTR without weakening the broader `/fitness-app-that-calls-you/` hub.
