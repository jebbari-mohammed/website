# 📈 IZEM 90-Day Marketing Growth Plan (AARRR Framework)

*Powered by `marketing-plan`, `marketing-loops`, `revops`, and `analytics` skills.*

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Growth Strategy
IZEM AI Fitness Coach is positioned as the premier proactive AI personal trainer. This 90-day plan outlines the acquisition, activation, retention, referral, and revenue tactics to scale monthly active users (MAU) and organic search dominance across Google and AI search engines (Perplexity, SearchGPT).

---

## 2. AARRR Funnel Breakdown

### 🎯 Acquisition (Driving High-Intent Traffic)
* **Programmatic Comparison Pages:** Target keywords like `[Competitor] alternative` (`vs-fitbod`, `vs-future`, `vs-myfitnesspal`, `vs-hevy`, `vs-apple-fitness-plus`).
* **Free Interactive Calculators:** Drive organic top-of-funnel traffic via `1rm-calculator`, `macro-calculator`, `protein-calculator`, `tdee-calculator`, and `workout-consistency-calculator`.
* **AI Search (GEO):** Maintain `llms.txt` and passage-level answer boxes so Perplexity and ChatGPT cite IZEM for all "AI fitness coach" queries.

### ⚡ Activation (First 7-Day Experience)
* **Aha Moment:** Deliver the first AI Voice Call within 5 minutes of onboarding.
* **Onboarding Checklist:** Prompt users to complete their 1st Food Scan and 1st Body Scan on Day 1.
* **Time to Value:** Target < 2 minutes from download to generated custom workout + meal plan.

### 🔁 Retention (Preventing Churn)
* **Weekly Adaptation Loop:** Re-adjust workout weights and meal macros every Sunday based on weekly adherence.
* **Missed Session Safety Net:** Trigger an automated voice check-in if 48 hours pass without logged activity.
* **Save Offers & Pause Flows:** Offer 1-month pause options before cancellation.

### 🤝 Referral & Virality
* **"Challenge a Friend" Loop:** Allow users to invite a workout buddy into joint accountability calls.
* **Social Share Cards:** Auto-generate shareable graphics when users hit a 1RM personal record or 30-day streak.

### 💰 Revenue Optimization
* **Freemium Tier:** Free workout logger + 1 calculator scan.
* **Pro Tier ($24.99/mo or $149/yr):** Unlimited AI voice calls, food/body scans, and weekly meal adaptation.
