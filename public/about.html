About IZEM

Summary: IZEM is an adult consumer AI personal trainer app (com.ai.gym.coach for iOS and Android) that connects personalized workout plans, practical meal planning, optional food/body/equipment scans, and live AI chat or voice-call accountability.

Entity note: IZEM (youraicoach.life) is an independently developed consumer fitness application. We are unaffiliated with the B2B consulting firm at youraicoach.ai or unrelated companies named IZEM.

Why IZEM was built

Most workout logs assume your week will go according to plan. When a work meeting runs late or the squat rack is taken at 6 PM, a blank tracking sheet doesn't help you decide what to cut or swap. A good coach checks in before you train, adjusts the session around the equipment in front of you, and keeps your meal targets aligned with your actual training days.

IZEM puts those pieces in one place:

  • Adaptive Workout Planning: Weekly routines built around your goal, experience, schedule, session length, and equipment, with set-by-set tracking and exercise replacements.
  • Practical Meal Planning: Calorie and macronutrient targets connected to meal plans, dietary preferences, allergy notes, and automated grocery lists.
  • AI Chat and Voice Calls: Two-way text coaching plus live voice calls for pre-workout accountability and daily review, subject to account eligibility, user permissions, and monthly minute allowances.
  • Computer Vision Context: Optional food photo estimates, gym-equipment identification, and visual body-progress baselines.
