About IZEM

Note: IZEM (youraicoach.life) is a consumer fitness application. We are not affiliated with the B2B AI consulting firm operating at youraicoach.ai.

"Most fitness apps track what you did. IZEM helps you decide what to do next."

The Problem

Personal training works because it creates structure and accountability. A good trainer keeps track of you, adjusts the plan, helps with meals, and pushes you when motivation drops. But human coaching can cost hundreds per month, and many people still need daily follow-up between sessions.

Meanwhile, many fitness apps are passive trackers. They wait for the user to open the app, pick a workout, log food, and stay motivated alone. When real life changes, the plan often stays static.

The Solution

IZEM was built to solve this. It should feel less like a dashboard and more like a coach.

The AI coach can call your phone before training and at review moments. It creates workouts around your goal, body, schedule, equipment, level, recovery, and progress. It builds practical meals around macros, preferences, schedule, and lifestyle. It can use scans of food, body progress, and gym equipment so the plan has more context.

IZEM is positioned as a premium AI personal trainer at around $24.99/month, with an annual plan as the best-value option.

The Technology

IZEM is a full-stack product built from the ground up:

  • Voice Calls — Real VoIP calls via professional voice infrastructure with native phone integration
  • Coach Memory — Context from previous workouts, meals, check-ins, and user preferences
  • Weekly Adaptation — The plan can change based on consistency, progress, recovery, schedule, and behavior
  • Progressive Training Logic — Workouts can adjust over time instead of staying static
  • Practical Nutrition — Meal planning around macros, preferences, schedule, and lifestyle
  • Computer Vision — Food, body progress, and gym equipment scanning for more context
  • Responsible Guidance — General fitness and nutrition support with safe, non-medical language

The Mission

To give serious users a premium coaching system that follows up, adapts, and makes the next step clear without the cost of traditional personal training.

IZEM is available on iOS and Android.
