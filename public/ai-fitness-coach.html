How the AI Coach Works

Step 1: Tell the Coach About You

Complete onboarding about your goals, fitness level, body metrics, preferences, injuries, available equipment, and schedule. Then use scans where helpful so the coach has more context.

Step 2: Get Your Personalized Program

The AI generates a workout program and meal plan for your real constraints. It explains the plan in plain language and updates it as your progress and life change.

Step 3: Train with 24/7 Support

Follow your program, scan food or equipment when useful, chat with IZEM, and receive proactive voice calls. The coach calls before sessions and reviews progress at the end of the day so the next step stays clear.

Why weekly adaptation matters

A plan that ignores missed workouts, bad sleep, schedule changes, and inconsistent meals becomes useless fast. IZEM is built to close that loop. It reviews the week, keeps the useful parts, and adjusts the next plan so consistency stays realistic.

  • Consistency review - What happened this week and where did the plan break?
  • Workout adjustment - Volume, exercises, equipment, and recovery can shift.
  • Meal adjustment - Food planning can adapt around schedule, preferences, and progress.
  • Accountability calls - Follow-up happens before motivation disappears.
  • Scan context - Food, body progress, and equipment scans can make the plan smarter.

Responsible guidance

IZEM provides general fitness and nutrition guidance, not medical advice. The app is designed to use safety-aware nutrition language and practical recommendations. Always consult a qualified professional before changing exercise or diet if you have a medical condition, injury, or clinical concern.
