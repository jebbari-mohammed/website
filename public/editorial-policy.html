Editorial and Corrections Policy

Accountable publisher: Mohammed Jebbari, founder of IZEM.

AI assistance

IZEM may use generative AI to help create a draft, organize material, check structure, or translate text. We disclose that practice. We do not give an AI system a human biography, claim it personally tested a product, or publish invented interviews, studies, user counts, credentials, or results.

Product and comparison claims

  • Product facts should match current IZEM behavior or current official documentation.
  • Competitor facts should come from that competitor's current official material whenever possible.
  • Prices are dated or described as approximate because they can change.
  • Examples and hypothetical scenarios are labeled and are not presented as testimonials.
  • Fitness and nutrition content is general information, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Corrections

If you find an inaccurate claim, broken source, or misleading passage, email support@youraicoach.life with the page URL and the problem. Material corrections should be made promptly; a page's modified date should change only when its content changed substantially.
