Editorial, Methodology and Corrections Policy

1. AI assistance and authorship disclosure

IZEM may use generative AI to assist with drafting, organizing structured tables, checking code or markup, or translating text. We do not assign a fictional human biography to an AI system, claim first-hand laboratory testing that did not occur, or publish invented interviews, clinical trials, user counts, credentials, or testimonials.

2. Calculator formulas and exercise-science methodology

Public calculators and planning hubs on youraicoach.life run directly in the browser using published equations from peer-reviewed nutrition and strength-conditioning literature. All outputs are educational starting estimates for healthy adults.

3. Product, pricing and comparison claims

  • Product accuracy: Descriptions of IZEM must match current application behavior (com.ai.gym.coach on iOS and Android), including voice-call allowances, workout and meal adaptation, and optional scan workflows.
  • Storefront pricing: IZEM is a members-only application after onboarding. Because regional prices, currencies, and introductory trial offers depend on the live Apple App Store or Google Play storefront, editorial pages do not quote unverified fixed prices.
  • Competitor comparisons: Pages such as IZEM vs Fitbod evaluate verifiable product architecture and publicly documented capabilities without disparagement or fabricated benchmarks.
  • Visual integrity: Public illustrations and diagrams follow IZEM's objects-only visual policy (equipment, interfaces, and data charts rather than synthetic human stock photography or implied before-and-after body transformations).
  • Non-medical scope: Fitness and nutrition material is general educational information for adults and never replaces a physician, registered dietitian, physical therapist, or in-person strength coach.

4. Corrections and updates

If you spot an inaccurate claim, broken citation, or calculation bug, email support@youraicoach.life with the page URL and description. Material corrections are verified against primary sources and repository tests before release, and a page's modified date changes only when its substance is updated.
