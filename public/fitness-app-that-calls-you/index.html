Why calls can work better than notifications

A push notification is easy to ignore because it asks for far less than a human coach emotionally. A call is different. It feels more like a commitment. You either answer, reject, or reschedule. That small moment of friction is useful because it makes the plan real again.

That does not mean calls should be annoying. A good accountability call should be timely, short, personal, and useful. It should remind you what today's workout is, acknowledge the context of your week, and help you start with the lowest possible resistance.

Example: "You planned upper body today. You slept badly last night, so keep the first two sets lighter and aim to show up instead of chasing a record. Ten minutes from now, shoes on."
