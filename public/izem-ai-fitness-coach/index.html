IZEM AI Fitness Coach: current features, trial, calls and limits

IZEM connects personalized workouts, real training logs, nutrition, scans, progress, AI chat and live voice calls around one coaching profile. This page states what the current product supports and where availability or eligibility still depends on the storefront and account.

Short answer: IZEM is an adult-only AI fitness coaching app built for iOS and Android. It is not only a calling app: the same profile can connect workout planning and execution, nutrition, progress, scans, AI chat, and eligible live voice calls.

Membership, 7-day trial and free test call

After onboarding, IZEM is a members-only product rather than a permanent free tier. The current onboarding flow gives an eligible non-member two ways to evaluate it:

Start the 7-day free trial

The app reads the introductory offer from the storefront. It shows the free-trial CTA only when the offer is zero-price and that specific store account is confirmed eligible.

Test one free coach call first

Instead of starting the trial immediately, an eligible non-member can test one complimentary onboarding coach call. The connected call is capped at 5 minutes; an unanswered ring may be retried.

The public website currently references a $24.99/month premium membership. The final App Store or Google Play offer shown to the user is authoritative for exact price, currency, renewal period, trial eligibility, annual options and purchase terms.

AI chat and live coach calls

IZEM includes continuing AI text chat and live two-way voice conversations. Supported conversations can use relevant context such as the current workout, meal plan, logged meals, recent performance, useful saved preferences, previous supported call summaries, recent changes and separately consented Health context when available.

You can call the coach

Premium members can start a live coach call from the app instead of waiting for the coach to call first.

The coach can call you

Optional coach-initiated calls can support planned-workout accountability and review moments when the account, platform, allowance and call preferences permit.

Up to 350 voice minutes/month

The current IZEM Elite paywall advertises up to 350 live voice-coaching minutes each month, with focused calls up to 15 minutes.

Coach intensity control

A user-controlled setting changes tone, directness, pacing and emotional intensity.

Coach memory and continuity

Useful saved preferences, current plans, recent performance and prior supported call summaries can provide continuity instead of treating every conversation as a blank slate.

Changes remain controlled

Supported exercise, workout-day and meal changes can be handled through coaching flows. Larger or destructive changes should be previewed and confirmed before they are committed.

Workout planning and execution

IZEM is built around an executable weekly training plan rather than a one-off workout generator. Plan inputs can include goals, experience level, available equipment, schedule, session duration and supplied constraints.

Important limit: IZEM does not claim to measure bar velocity and does not provide hands-on exercise-form supervision. Video references and setup cues are educational aids, not a substitute for qualified in-person coaching where technique or safety requires it.

Nutrition, allergies, meal changes and grocery lists

Nutrition is part of the same coaching profile. Meal-plan inputs include dietary preference, allergies, meal count and calorie/macro targets.

Personalized meal plans

Meal plans use the nutrition preferences and targets supplied by the user.

Allergy context

Allergies are explicit profile inputs used by meal-generation and supported meal-replacement flows.

Meal logging and changes

Meals can be logged, and supported meals can be replaced or adjusted while relevant constraints stay in context.

Generated grocery list

The active meal plan generates a grocery list inside the nutrition workflow.

Food-photo estimates

A food photo can produce estimated calories and macros. These are estimates, not laboratory measurements.

Medical-diet boundary

Ingredient labels and qualified professional guidance take priority when an allergy or medical dietary requirement makes precision safety-critical.

Scans and progress

Optional camera tools add coaching context. They are not presented as clinical measurement devices.

  • Food scan: estimated calories and macros from a meal photo.
  • Gym-equipment scan: equipment identification with general setup context and exercise ideas.
  • Body-progress scan: an approximate visual baseline and trend signal, not a clinical body-composition test.
  • Weight progress: weight logging with history/trend views.
  • Training consistency: the progress experience includes a current training streak and recorded workout evidence.

Apple Health: exact scope and consent

On supported iPhones, Apple Health is optional and permission-based. IZEM can read a bounded overview after the user separately chooses to allow access. When available, supported categories include:

StepsExercise minutesSleepWorkoutsResting heart rateHRVBody mass

Health access is read-only. Missing or denied data is not treated as zero. A minimized Health summary can support relevant coaching only after separate consent.

Health boundary: Apple Health context supports general fitness coaching. It is not diagnosis, treatment, emergency monitoring or medical advice.

FAQ

Does IZEM have a 7-day free trial?

Yes, for store accounts that the current storefront confirms are eligible for the zero-price one-week introductory offer.

Can I test the AI call before subscribing?

Yes. At the end of onboarding, an eligible non-member can choose one complimentary coach test call instead of starting the trial immediately. The connected call is capped at 5 minutes.

Can I call the coach myself?

Yes. Premium members can start a live coach call. Optional coach-initiated calls can also support planned workouts and review moments when enabled and eligible.

How many voice minutes are included?

The current IZEM Elite paywall advertises up to 350 live voice-coaching minutes each month, with focused calls up to 15 minutes.

Does IZEM track sets, reps and load?

Yes. It supports set-by-set repetitions and load tracking, workout history, effort feedback, and full or partial workout logging.

Does the meal planner account for allergies?

Yes. Allergies and dietary preference are explicit nutrition profile inputs. Food-photo estimates do not replace checking ingredient labels when allergy safety matters.

Does IZEM make a grocery list?

Yes. The active meal plan generates a grocery list inside the nutrition workflow.

What does Apple Health add?

With separate permission on supported iPhones, available steps/exercise minutes, sleep, workouts, resting heart rate, HRV and body mass can provide bounded fitness context.
