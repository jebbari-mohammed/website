Superset

Definition: Performing two exercises back-to-back with no rest in between. Can target the same muscle (compound set) or opposing muscles (antagonist superset). Increases workout efficiency and metabolic demand.

Detailed Explanation

A superset involves performing two exercises back-to-back with minimal rest. This increases workout density, saves time, and boosts cardiovascular demand.

Practical Application & Guide

Pair non-competing muscle groups (e.g. chest press followed by a row) to save time in the gym without sacrificing the performance of either lift.

Common Pitfalls & Mistakes

Supersetting heavy, systemically demanding compound lifts (like squats and deadlifts), which severely impairs performance and form.
