If you loathe spending your Sunday afternoons hovering over a boiling pot of chicken breasts, you aren't alone. Last Wednesday, I sat down at 8:00 PM with zero energy to cook. My phone buzzed. How can you eat for performance without cooking for hours?

The secret isn't a complex meal prep system; it's about assembly rather than cooking. You can swap out elaborate recipes for high-quality, pre-cooked staples that meet your macros. Think rotisserie chickens, bags of pre-washed greens, and microwaveable quinoa pouches.

If you use an AI fitness coach, it can actually learn which foods you don't mind prepping versus those you despise. By scanning your meals with your camera, the coach gains context on your preferences, ensuring your nutrition plan isn't filled with meals that will just end up rotting in the back of your fridge.

How do you keep nutrition consistent when you're busy?

Consistency isn't about eating perfectly; it's about having a safety net.
