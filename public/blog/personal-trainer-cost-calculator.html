The COACH fit test

Cost comparison is useful only after you score the coaching job. Use COACH before paying for a package or switching to an app.

C

Correction

Do you need someone in the room to watch movement, adjust setup, or spot a lift?

O

Outreach

Do you need the coach to reach you before the workout window closes?

A

Adaptation

Will the plan change after missed sessions, low energy, travel, or equipment limits?

C

Context

Are workouts, meals, scans, schedule, and recurring blockers connected?

H

Habit

Does the system make the next repeatable action clearer after an imperfect day?

Worked example: when cheaper is not the same as better

Situation: your local trainer quote is two sessions per week. The math is simple, but the decision is not.

If the blocker is form: a few human sessions may be the smartest first purchase because you need live feedback.

If the blocker is consistency: the better comparison is whether the system reaches you before the skip, gives a fallback workout, adapts meals, and reviews the day.

If both are true: use a human for technique and a coaching app for the days between sessions.

Questions to ask before you pay anyone

  • What exact support is included outside the paid session or app screen?
  • What happens if you miss two workouts in the same week?
  • Can the plan adjust when equipment is unavailable?
  • Does meal planning fit your real food preferences and schedule?
  • Is the coach or app honest about injuries, pain, medical issues, and form limits?
  • Can you explain why this option solves your actual failure point?
