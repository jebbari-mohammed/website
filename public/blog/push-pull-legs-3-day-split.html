How 3-day PPL works: You train three non-consecutive days per week—Push (chest, shoulders, triceps), Pull (back, rear delts, biceps), and Legs (quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves). Because each workout happens once every seven days, working sets must be taken within 1 to 2 Reps in Reserve (RIR) with 9 to 12 weekly sets per muscle group [1] [2].

Complete 3-day Push / Pull / Legs workout tables

In a 3-day PPL routine, you have a full rest day after every session. That allows you to place two multi-joint presses on Push day, two heavy pulls on Pull day, and both a squat and hip hinge on Leg day without carrying fatigue into tomorrow's workout.

The honest limitation of 3-day PPL (and how to fix it)

Originally, Push/Pull/Legs was designed to run twice across six days (Push, Pull, Legs, Push, Pull, Legs, Rest). When you run it on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday, each muscle group waits seven full days before it gets trained again. Resistance-training meta-analyses show that hitting a muscle twice per week outperforms once-weekly training when total sets aren't equated—and makes it easier to perform high-quality sets before fatigue sets in [1].

If you only have three gym days per week, you have two ways to keep PPL effective:

  1. The Micro-Bridge Option (stay on 3 days/week): Add one 3-set touch-up movement at the end of each day for a lagging muscle group—for example, 3 sets of Chin-Ups at the end of Push day, 3 sets of Goblet or Split Squats at the end of Pull day, and 3 sets of Incline Dumbbell Presses or Push-Ups at the end of Leg day. That gives chest, back, and quads a second weekly stimulus in five extra minutes.
  2. The Rolling Every-Other-Day PPL (3.5 days/week): Train Monday, Wednesday, Friday, Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday. Each muscle is trained once every 6 days instead of every 7 days.

Frequently asked questions

Can beginners build muscle on a 3-day Push Pull Legs split?

Yes. As long as total weekly volume reaches roughly 9 to 12 hard sets per major muscle group (taken within 1 to 2 RIR) and you progressively add repetitions or weight over time, a 3-day PPL split builds both strength and muscle.

Should I put deadlifts on Pull day or Leg day in a 3-day PPL?

In a Monday/Wednesday/Friday PPL schedule, putting Romanian Deadlifts on Friday (Leg day) and keeping Wednesday (Pull day) focused on vertical pulls and chest-supported rows prevents your lower back from getting taxed twice in 48 hours.

What if I miss Friday's Leg day on a 3-day PPL?

Start next Monday with Legs, then run Push on Wednesday and Pull on Friday. Rotating the three workouts in order prevents your lower body from going two full weeks without training.
