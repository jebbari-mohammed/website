Privacy, in plain language

Your data should stay yours.

This policy explains what IZEM collects, when it uses Google Gemini for coaching, and which controls remain in your hands.

Effective July 22, 2026 · Last updated July 22, 2026

Important before you continue

IZEM is an adult-only service. You must be 18 or older. Before any personal information is sent to Google Gemini for AI coaching, IZEM asks for a separate, versioned permission in the app. You can revoke it in Profile at any time; AI features then stop until you choose again.

1. What IZEM uses

AccountName, email, sign-in provider, age requirement, subscription entitlement, and account identifiers used to authenticate you and provide membership access.

Coaching profileGoals, training experience, equipment, schedule, body measurements you enter, food preferences, plans, progress logs, and selected settings.

Content you choose to sendChat messages, live or recorded voice content, bounded call-continuity records, feedback, photos selected for meal, equipment, body-scan, or chat analysis, and—only after a separate permission—a minimized Apple Health summary for the coaching uses described below. Raw selected photos, raw voice recordings, and raw Health samples are not retained on IZEM servers.

OperationsPush/VoIP device tokens, product interactions, purchase history, usage counters, security events, and limited diagnostics needed to deliver calls, enforce limits, prevent abuse, and keep the app reliable.

We use these categories for app functionality, personalization you request, security, support, billing entitlement, and reliability. We do not sell personal information, use it for cross-app tracking, or run advertising in IZEM.

IZEM may use observable workout-completion patterns and coarse call-engagement signals—such as call duration, turn balance, or an early end—to make coaching less repetitive and lower pressure when an approach is not landing. These signals are not used to diagnose you or treated as proof of your mood, personality, motive, or intent, and they are never used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

2. AI processing and Google Gemini

When you grant the in-app AI-processing permission and use an AI feature, relevant information—such as your coaching profile, prompt, selected photo, voice-call audio, or plan context—is sent to Google’s paid Gemini API service. IZEM uses Gemini to create fitness plans, answer coaching conversations, analyze selected images, and power live voice coaching.

IZEM uses paid Gemini credentials only. Google’s paid-service terms say prompts and outputs are not used to improve Google products, while its current abuse-monitoring policy says prompts, context, and outputs are retained for 55 days for safety, security, and required legal or regulatory disclosures; flagged content may be reviewed by authorized personnel. IZEM does not opt into sharing Gemini logs, datasets, or feedback for Google product improvement. See Google’s Gemini API abuse-monitoring policy, Google’s logging and sharing policy, and Google’s terms for the provider’s current terms.

IZEM stores the account data needed to make the product work, including plans, conversation records, scan analyses, bounded thread summaries, and carefully validated coaching memories. Raw selected photos, raw voice recordings, raw Apple Health samples, and the minimized Health snapshot are not retained by IZEM servers. A Health-influenced chat reply may remain in conversation history and a workout change you confirm may persist with coarse provenance; the Health values themselves are not saved with either. To show a photo or voice note back inside its chat, the app may keep an account-scoped copy in the device’s non-backed-up cache; those copies may be evicted by the operating system and are removed when you sign out or delete the account. If you use Sign in with Apple, IZEM stores a server-only Apple refresh credential solely so the app can revoke its Apple authorization when you delete the account; the credential is never exposed to the app and is removed with the account. You can delete your IZEM account from Profile; account deletion revokes linked Sign in with Apple authorization and removes IZEM-held account records and server-owned identifiers, subject to narrowly scoped legal or fraud-prevention records where retention is required.

3. Apple Health is optional and has two separate controls

On-device dashboard

If you connect Apple Health, IZEM requests read-only access to the categories you select, which may include steps, Apple Exercise Time, sleep analysis, workouts, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability (SDNN), and body mass. The dashboard is built on your iPhone from bounded recent aggregates. IZEM does not upload or retain that dashboard overview, use it for ads or analytics, or write to Apple Health. Missing data is shown as unavailable—not as zero.

“Use Health with my coach”

This is a separate, default-off permission. If you enable it, you choose categories and IZEM creates a minimized summary on your device: rounded recent patterns and latest recorded sleep duration, coverage and freshness, personal-baseline bands, broad workout types, and (if selected) a coarse body-weight range. It excludes raw samples, exact source measurements, exact timestamps, routes, source devices, identifiers, and HealthKit permission states.

The separate permission covers only coach chats you start, voice calls after you answer, and workout suggestions or adjustments you explicitly request. Health data never makes an automatic plan change, creates a readiness score, or silently becomes a durable goal, commitment, preference, or coach memory.

For an answered voice call, the minimized summary goes directly from your iPhone to Google’s paid Gemini service; nothing is shared while the call is ringing. For a user-initiated coach chat or an explicitly requested workout suggestion or adjustment, the summary is processed transiently in memory by an IZEM Firebase Cloud Function and then sent to Google’s paid Gemini service. IZEM does not write the summary to its database, logs, analytics, crash reports, support exports, thread summaries, or coach memory.

A Health-influenced reply remains in your conversation history with a coarse provenance marker, but is excluded from later model conversation history, thread summaries, and automatic coach-memory extraction. A workout change persists only after you request or confirm it, with coarse provenance that Health context informed the change; the summary and Health values are not stored with the plan.

IZEM may keep only the minimized summary in encrypted, device-only Keychain storage so it is ready without delaying a request or an answered scheduled call. A summary up to 12 hours old is treated as current; a summary more than 12 and no more than 36 hours old is explicitly treated as stale; after 36 hours it is never used and is deleted the next time IZEM checks it. Turning off Health coaching, disconnecting Apple Health, signing out, or deleting your account deletes the cached summary immediately. Health-assisted calls do not create a saved coach-memory episode or use Gemini Live session resumption.

Turning Health coaching off stops future Health use and clears the local summary. It cannot undo Google’s applicable 55-day abuse-monitoring retention, remove an existing chat reply, or reverse a workout change you already confirmed. Apple Health context is for general fitness and wellness guidance—not diagnosis, a readiness score, medical clearance, emergency care, or a replacement for a clinician. Apple controls the underlying permission in the Health app. Revoking IZEM access does not delete records held by Apple Health.

4. Service providers

  • Google Firebase: authentication, Firestore data storage, Cloud Functions, push delivery, security, and service reliability. IZEM does not use Firebase Analytics in the release app.
  • Google Gemini: the paid AI processor described above, only after your AI permission.
  • RevenueCat and Apple / Google Play: subscription presentation, purchase processing, entitlement validation, restores, and billing management. IZEM does not receive your full payment card number.
  • Sentry: limited crash and performance diagnostics, configured to avoid message, photo, audio, Health, and direct prompt content.
  • YouTube: optional embedded and external exercise or cooking videos. If you choose to play or open one, YouTube may receive network, device, and video-interaction information under Google’s own privacy terms. IZEM uses privacy-enhanced embeds where available and does not use video activity for advertising or cross-app tracking.

5. Retention and your controls

We keep account and coaching records while your account is active or until you remove them through available product controls. Older conversation content may be summarized into a bounded thread summary and the older raw messages may be pruned as part of storage controls. Scrubbed crash diagnostics retained directly by IZEM expire after 30 days; feedback records are retained for a limited period for safety and quality review. Operational and abuse-prevention records are minimized and access-controlled.

You can (a) revoke AI processing in Profile, (b) separately revoke Health coaching or disconnect Apple Health, (c) start a new chat thread, (d) restore or manage purchases through the storefront, and (e) delete your account in Profile. Revocation stops future processing but cannot recall information already within a provider’s disclosed retention period. Deleting an IZEM account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; manage that separately in the storefront. It also cannot delete Apple Health records.

To report an unsafe response, use the message report action or contact support@youraicoach.life. For privacy questions or a deletion issue, contact us from the email associated with your account.

6. Security, transfers, and changes

IZEM uses Firebase security rules, App Check, server-side entitlement and consent checks, encrypted device storage for sensitive local tokens, and transport encryption. No online service can promise absolute security. Data may be processed in countries where our providers operate, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

We may update this policy when the product or law changes. The in-app AI disclosure is versioned; material changes require a new choice before AI processing resumes. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
